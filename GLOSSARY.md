# Glossary

Domain terms for the Material Register. No implementation details here.

- **Material**: one catalogue record for a part, consumable, raw material or tool, held at one plant. Identified by its material number.
- **Material number**: the unique identifier of a material, such as `MAT-000123`.
- **Material type**: the kind of material: Spare Part, Consumable, Raw Material or Tool.
- **Status**: where a material is in its life: Active (can be used), Obsolete (no longer used or ordered) or Pending Review (awaiting a decision).
- **Plant**: a site that stocks materials, identified by a 4-digit code and a name, such as `1000 · Brisbane`.
- **Manufacturer**: the company that makes a material, such as SKF.
- **MPN (manufacturer part number)**: the manufacturer's own identifier for the part, such as `6205-2RS1`.
- **Unit of measure (UoM)**: the unit stock and price are counted in, such as EA (each), KG or L.
- **Stock on hand**: the quantity of a material currently held at its plant.
- **Maintenance planner**: the user: someone finding the right part quickly, for example "all active bearings from SKF stocked at the Brisbane plant".
